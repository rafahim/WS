## Purchase flow

Every product card and product detail page provides a Buy / Continue to payment action. It opens a product-specific payment status page which clearly states that the item is not available for purchase yet. No payment form, order submission or payment success state exists. Visitors can return to the product or contact Wishly at https://wishly.bd/contact.

## Collection image direction

Each collection image now matches its category: Wishly Cards use a digital letter preview, Mystery Gifts use a sealed question-mark gift box, Birthday Gifts use a birthday page/cake preview, Anniversary Gifts use a memory-map design, and Friendship Gifts use a friends-together visual. Product pages use their own matching Wishly template or gift artwork rather than reusing unrelated category images.
